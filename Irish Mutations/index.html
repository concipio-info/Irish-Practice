Sounds, spelling and scope

Séimhiú ≈ “SHAY-voo”; urú ≈ “uh-ROO”. These are rough English-friendly guides. Lenition changes a consonant’s sound; the h is not a separate English “h”. In eclipsis the added initial represents the new sound: for example, mb is pronounced as m. Fh is normally silent.

Brackets group words that belong together. Only enter the missing word, preserving fadas. Capitalisation, outer spaces and curly apostrophes are tolerated. H-, t- and vowel n-prefixes are distinguished in “Identify the change”.

This is a practical core course, not an exhaustive grammar. It uses standard written forms and the central/eclipsis pattern in labelled preposition + article exercises. Munster and other dialects have additional patterns, notably with sa/san; those variable forms are not tested here. Case forms such as fir are supplied: you practise the initial, not the ending.
