HOW TO USE BRIATHRA

Small steps. A broad foundation.

What to type

Enter the complete verb phrase: particle + verb + subject, where needed. The rest of the sentence is supplied. A synthetic form already includes its subject: molaim, not molaim mé. All alternatives shown in the reference table are accepted. Capitalisation, final punctuation, extra spaces and curly apostrophes do not affect the score.

Coverage

All 11 commonly taught irregular verbs, plus 50 useful regular verbs spanning both conjugations. The regular list is a curated learning selection, not a verified corpus-ranked top 50. “Regular” describes finite conjugation: verbal nouns can still be unpredictable.

Finite forms: present/habitual present, the distinct present of bí, past, habitual past, future, conditional, imperative, present subjunctive and hypothetical past-subjunctive clauses with dá/mura. All seven personal pronouns and the autonomous form are included where applicable. Statements, negatives, questions and negative questions are available for indicative and conditional forms; commands and wishes do not use question forms.

Additional practice covers present, past, future, conditional and habitual-past progressive constructions; present, past, future and conditional perfect constructions with tar éis; verbal nouns and verbal adjectives. These compound constructions are combinations of bí with a verbal noun, not extra inflectional tenses. Progressive bí is excluded. Nonfinite forms are single vocabulary cards and do not vary by person.

Examples & grammatical groups

Brackets mark the verb phrase and its complement, for example [Scríobh mé] [an litir]. After a verbal noun, a definite object commonly changes to the genitive: [Tá mé ag scríobh] [na litreach]. English examples explain the intended meaning; autonomous forms are glossed with “someone” for practice, though their natural translation depends on context.

Dialect & pronunciation

The tables follow the forms supplied by BuNaMo and Gramadán and accept their listed analytic and synthetic alternatives. They are not an exhaustive inventory of dialectal, emphatic or literary forms. Some synthetic alternatives are especially useful in Munster. The lemma pronunciation hints are rough English-friendly guides, not full phonetic transcriptions or audio. Use each verb's Teanglann link for dialect recordings when online.

The copula is is a separate construction and is not counted among the 11 verbs. Relative clauses, reported-speech particles and every possible aspectual idiom are outside this package.

Offline & privacy

Unzip the whole folder and open index.html. No installation, server, account or API key is needed. Progress stays in this browser when storage is available. Some browsers restrict storage for local files; use Export progress as a backup. Only dictionary and source links need internet access.
