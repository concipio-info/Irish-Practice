IRISH CLAUSE CONNECTORS — v1.0

Unzip this folder, then double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge,
Firefox or Safari. No internet connection, server or installation is needed.

82 original teaching examples in 22 connector families.
Choose a missing phrase, type a missing phrase, or build a sentence from words.
Open Reference & examples for grammar notes and bracketed sentence structures.
Click Next question after checking an answer. Scores and mistakes are saved
in your browser when local storage is available. Review mistakes to try them again.

Typing ignores case, punctuation and extra spaces. Fadas are required by default;
this can be switched off. The on-screen accent buttons insert á é í ó ú.
Exercises check supplied model constructions, not all possible translations.

Broad coverage of common constructions, including tense, negative and copular
variations. This is not an exhaustive catalogue of every dialect or literary form.
Pronunciation hints are approximate and cover connectors, not whole sentences.

The MIT licence is included inside index.html under How to use.
Optional dictionary reference links need internet. No external resources are
loaded automatically. No personal data is transmitted.

To put this in an app launcher, place the folder alongside your other apps and
link to Irish-Clause-Connectors/index.html (adjust the path if renamed).
