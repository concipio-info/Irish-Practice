Quick reference · the central standard pattern
  • de + an → den · den chrann
  • do + an → don · don fhear
  • faoi + an → faoin · faoin ngrian
  • i + an → sa / san · sa bhosca; san fharraige
  • ó + an → ón · ón gcarr
  • le + an → leis an · leis an mbean
  • trí + an → tríd an · tríd an bhfuinneog
  • gan + an → gan an · gan an t-uisce

After den, don, sa/san, a following singular consonant usually lenites; after most other listed prepositions with an, it usually eclipses. Singular d and t stay unchanged, a feminine s normally gains t, and vowels stay unchanged. Gan an instead keeps the nominative article pattern. The Caighdeán also permits eclipsis after den/don/sa; these cards teach the central pattern.
