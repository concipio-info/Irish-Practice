Abair Ar Ais

Irish answers a yes-or-no question by echoing its verb. Hear or read the question, then give the affirmative or negative reply about yourself. Choose or type the complete reply.

The question says tú (“you”); reply as mé (“I”). In the present tense, the “I” ending is often built into the verb. Accents matter in typed mode; punctuation and case do not.

How echo answers work

An bhfuil tú réidh? → Tá / Níl. For a question addressed to you, Táim / Nílim (“I am / I am not”) is also accepted here. Other verbs follow the question’s tense but may change their stem: An ndearna tú é? → Rinne mé / Ní dhearna mé. The latter answers mean “I did / I did not.” Rinneadh is an impersonal form (“was done”), so it is not the reply to “Did you do it?”

Audio uses your browser’s speech synthesis. A Gaeilge voice is used when one is installed; pronunciation can vary by device. All questions and answers work offline without audio.
